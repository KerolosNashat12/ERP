Brand rail: one frame for every logo, whatever shape it arrives in

THE BUG (from the owner's screenshot of «ماركاتنا»)
The rail was a skyline, then two overlapping rows with a black rectangle
hanging out of the second one. Two causes, both in public/shop/shop.css:

1. TWO FACE SIZES. A brand WITH a logo got its own 150x100 box; a brand
   without one collapsed to the size of the little drawn ring, because the
   night skin set that face to inline-size: auto. Two face sizes make two
   card heights, and a flex row of mismatched cards wraps.
2. A MARK BIGGER THAN ITS FRAME. The logo was held inside the frame by
   max-inline-size / max-block-size: 100%. Those never resolved: the face
   is a grid with place-items: center, so the item's area is content-sized,
   the percentage is indefinite, and the browser ignores the cap. The drawn
   fallback rendered at its intrinsic 150x150 inside an 88px frame and was
   sliced off top and bottom by overflow: hidden. Percentage caps were tried
   twice before this was measured properly.

THE FIX
The frame is declared once, for every state at once - .has-logo, the drawn
fallback and the plain letter - as two custom properties on .brand-card-face.
Absolute lengths always resolve, and they inherit, so everything inside the
frame is sized from the same two numbers. A skin changes those two numbers
and the mark follows: classic keeps the 104px circle, luxe uses a 132x88
rounded rectangle so a wordmark is readable. Nothing below may set the width
or height of a face again. A logo is CONTAINED, never cropped - cutting the
edge off somebody's trademark is worse than leaving air around it. A square
logo gets 16px of padding so the classic circle does not clip its corners.
Two duplicate copies of the same night selector, which disagreed about the
hairline ring, are now one.

HOW IT IS PINNED
tests/brand-rail-ui-check.mjs uploads deliberately hostile logos - 900x200,
200x900 and 500x500 - and measures the real page in a browser across classic
and luxe, Arabic and English, desktop and phone: one face size, one card
size, one row, and no mark larger than its frame. Putting the original CSS
back fails it six times, with both symptoms named.

ALSO
- The stylesheet had the shared .tile-badge, .brand-card-face selector list
  split by a comment sitting between its two selectors. Valid CSS, but it
  hid the rule from the check that keeps a category tile and a brand face
  the same object. Comment moved above the list; the check now also asserts
  the face takes its width and height FROM the shared frame rather than
  hard-coding them, so the next skin cannot leave one face behind.
