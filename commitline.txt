COSTING (found by the owner's question about buying the same product twice)
This ERP values stock at MOVING WEIGHTED AVERAGE: one piece at 250 and one at
300 make two pieces at 275, and a sale costs 275. There are no purchase layers
to draw down, so "which PO is deducted first" has no answer - and does not
need one.

But asking it exposed a real bug. Each sale LINE stored the variant's standard
cost, which receiving a purchase order overwrites with the latest price, while
the sale HEADER stored the true average. On exactly his numbers:
  sales_summary     profit 25  (header)
  profit_and_costs  profit 25  (header)
  sales_by_product  profit  0  (line)   <- wrong
The report a shop opens to ask what a product earns was the one saying zero.
The line now carries the cost the stock movement actually took out, so all
three agree. Same shape as the 682-vs-673 disagreement found before.

Checked and CORRECT already, now pinned by tests: a void puts the piece back
at what it left at rather than at today's average; a customer return re-enters
at what it was sold at; issuing stock does not move the average; receiving a
dearer batch updates the standard cost and NEVER the shelf price.
