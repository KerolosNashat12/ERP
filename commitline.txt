The banner from the reference, and figures that are actually counted

BANNER
- The heading is a textarea now, so a shop can type its own line breaks. The
  SECOND line leans - "Accessories / That Define / Your Essence" - which is
  most of why the reference reads as an editorial. The CSS for it had existed
  for months with nothing able to produce it.
- A second button beside the first (Explore Collection / Our Story), set in
  Settings, and both now show over an uploaded photo instead of only over the
  gradient.
- FIX: the title clamp was fixed at 2 lines, so a three-line heading came back
  as "That Defin..." with the third line gone. It now follows what was typed,
  capped at 3.
- FIX (Arabic): line-height 1.06 is a Latin display setting and was slicing
  the tops off tashkeel. And font-style:italic on Arabic makes the browser
  SHEAR the glyphs, breaking the letter joins - Amiri has no italic face. The
  Arabic middle line takes the shop's accent colour instead.

THE FIGURES UNDER IT
Off by default. Switched on in Settings, and every number is counted from the
shop's own catalogue: published products, brands with something published, and
what delivery ACTUALLY costs - "Free shipping" is printed only by a shop that
gives it. Rounded DOWN with a "+" above 50 so the claim stays literally true;
exact below that. Works in both templates and both languages.
- FIX: the band's labels were at 3.5:1, under the floor for 12px text.
