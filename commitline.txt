THE ASK
"the logo is not right ... try to improve the colours and design a bit ...
and remove the 'an Egyptian software house - from Giza' line ...
whatsApp : 01121249801" - and, on the name: a real rename
from Nexora to KJEVORA SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, confirmed rather than assumed.

THE LOGO IS HIS OWN NOW
He sent the company's actual logo as a render on an office wall. The symbol is
cut out of that render to a transparent PNG and ships as the page's mark - in
the header, the footer and the tab icon. The cut is a FLOOD FILL from the
border rather than a luminance threshold, because the mark's own specular
highlights are as pale as the wall: a threshold punches holes through them, a
flood fill never reaches them. A first pass left a rim of pale wall pixels at
partial alpha, which at 46px reads as a light box behind the mark rather than
as a soft edge; any partially transparent pixel that is also pale is wall, not
artwork, and is dropped.

The second render he sent was tried first and abandoned: its wall is textured
concrete and its K is silver, so no single test separates them and the cut ate
half the letter. Two of his images, and only one of them is a usable source.

The banner he sent is the hero's background. Blurred and dropped to a third,
deliberately: at full strength the wall-mounted wordmark sits directly behind
the headline and the reader meets the company name twice, in two typefaces, at
once. It is atmosphere, not a second logo.

THE PAGE
Dark throughout rather than a dark hero on a white page, on the palette the
theme derives from the cyan in the mark. The three systems are a BENTO - the
lead system takes the whole top row and the two others share the one beneath,
which is the shape of the business rather than a layout whim. The "an
Egyptian software house - from Giza" line is gone from the eyebrow, as asked. `versus` and `audience`
are gone entirely - removed rather than switched off, because a section
switched off is still in the HTML and the no-JS page would show what the
rendered page hides. Sections gained a `kicker`, which is in the contract and
editable like any other copy rather than baked into the markup.

WhatsApp and phone are 01121249801 everywhere - the header button, both contact
cards, the closing band and the demo form's own wa.me action, which all move
together off the one field in the document.

DOCUMENT_VERSION 2 -> 3
Same mechanism as the 1 -> 2 bump two hours earlier, same reason: a document
saved from the console under the Nexora cut describes a page that no longer
exists, and merging it over the new defaults would put that name and those
words straight back over the top. He had in fact saved one.

THREE THINGS THE BROWSER CHECK CAUGHT, ALL REAL
 - A CASCADE COLLISION in the header. `.btn` sets `display: inline-flex` and is
   declared further down the sheet than `.head-cta { display: none }`, so the
   quote button stayed on a 390px header and pushed the page 49px sideways.
   Scoping the hide to `.head-actions .head-cta` wins on specificity instead of
   hoping for order.
 - `og:description` is written from `seo.description`, so the HTML has to ship
   exactly that string. It shipped a shorter social variant, and the render
   rewrote it on boot - a DOM mutation on a page that is supposed to perform
   none.
 - The hero's built-in fallback still pointed at a screen capture. It is the
   company's banner now, and one file for both languages: a photograph has no
   language.
