Shoot mode - photograph a catalogue without losing your place

The owner asked four times for product photographs to be downloaded from the
internet. They cannot be: pictures of a named product belong to whoever shot
them, and a generic stock bottle on a product page is worse than an empty
frame - a customer orders the thing in the picture and returns the thing in
the box. Three open-licensed sources were tried and all three are unreachable.

So the answer is to make HIS photographs cheap. The cost was never the
shooting, it was the navigation: find a bare product, open it, scroll to the
photo card, upload, go back, remember where you were, two hundred times.

Products -> Photo session. One product on the screen, a button, and the next
one, ordered by BRAND so the walk follows the shelves instead of scattering
one supplier through the list. Skipping moves on without marking anything
done, so a skipped product is offered again next time. The counter is the
whole job, not the page.
