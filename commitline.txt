Choose a size on the card instead of leaving the shelf

THE ASK
The owner, on a product card whose button read "choose the size":
"ينفع تظبط اختر المقاس دي ل اضف للسله عادي"

WHAT IT DID
A product with ONE size added in a tap. A product with several said "choose
the size" and took the shopper to the product page - honest, but it sent them
away from the shelf they were browsing to answer one question.

WHAT IT DOES NOW
Every card says "add to cart". A product with more than one size answers the
tap with its sizes, on the card, each with its own price; tapping one adds
THAT one. The shopper never leaves the shelf.

Two things it deliberately does not do:
- It does not guess. Adding the cheapest size blind is one tap shorter and
  puts the wrong bottle in the bag, found out at the door.
- It does not ask when there is nothing to ask. Four sizes with one of them in
  stock has no choice in it, so that one goes straight in.

The sizes are fetched the first time somebody opens a product's panel and kept
for the rest of the visit - a shelf is two dozen products and sending every
variant of every one of them would undo the performance round for a panel most
shoppers never open. The promise is cached, not the result, so two quick taps
share one request instead of racing two.

HOW IT IS PINNED
tests/quick-add-sizes-check.mjs builds its own fixture - a product with three
sizes at three prices and a product with one, both published and stocked -
then drives a real browser: every card is labelled "add to cart"; opening the
sizes leaves the basket EMPTY; choosing the 100ml at 750 puts the 100ml at 750
in the basket (the last one, so "it adds the first whatever you tapped" cannot
pass); and a one-size product adds on the first tap with no panel at all.

Reversion-checked three ways: putting the old label and navigation back fails
it, guessing the first size fails it, and adding the wrong size fails it by
name.

THREE THINGS THE FIRST CUT GOT WRONG, ALL FOUND BY RUNNING IT
- `api.get(...)` does not exist on the storefront's request object. The panel
  opened, said "loading", and stayed there. It is `api.product(id)` - the
  request door that knows the tenant prefix and de-duplicates in-flight calls.
- `.card-add` is a RULER the size of the photo and is pointer-events: none so
  the rest of the card stays clickable. The check clicked its centre, landed
  on the card's own link, navigated to the product page, and reported "no size
  panel" about a panel that worked.
- The check hard-crashed on a 30-second Playwright timeout when the panel was
  missing - which is exactly what a regression looks like - and died before
  printing any of its findings. It reports now.
