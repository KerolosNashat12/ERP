The figures band turns itself on for a shop that has something to say

The band shipped defaulted to OFF, on the reasoning that "12 PRODUCTS" reads
as an apology. That was right about the platform and wrong about the person
who asked for it: he published the release and the feature was invisible.

Migration 029 derives the value instead of imposing one, the same shape as
026: a shop with 20+ published products across 3+ brands gets the band; a
nearly-empty shop does not; and a shop that has already chosen keeps its
choice. Nobody has to know the setting exists to get the sensible answer.

Also: the rounding ladder jumped to 50s at 200, which turned a shop with 248
products into "200+" - true, and 48 products of false modesty on its own front
page. It now steps in 10s up to 1000, so 248 reads as "240+". The browser
check now fails if the band rounds more than 10% off the real figure.

And tests/photo-input.test.js now strips comments before scanning. It was
reading raw source, so the comment explaining why the camera attribute was
removed - which quotes the attribute - could have failed the test on a correct
file. It passed only because a backtick happened to precede that occurrence.
